The best time to buy depends on how many seats you need

1–2 tickets

Wait. Buy on game day.

Pairs stay available in nearly every game right up to puck drop, and the typical game's pair price barely moves. Check the market 3–6 hours before the game and take anything you like. There is no reward for buying days ahead, and no penalty for deciding at lunch on game day.

3–4 tickets

Buy game-day afternoon.

The sweet spot is 6 to 2 hours before the game: prices have settled, and blocks of four are almost always still on sale and fairly priced. Wait past that and the odds turn: by the final 30 minutes, waiting has backfired (block gone or ≥20% pricier) in about -% of games.

5–6 tickets

Buy early. A day ahead, or by T-6h at the latest.

Big contiguous blocks are consumed and never replenished. Waiting into the final hour backfires, the block gone or at least 20% pricier than it was at midday, in about -% of games. The best big-block deals of a game week usually appear days out and get picked off within hours.
